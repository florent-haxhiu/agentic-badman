# Phase 3: Supercharge Your Agents

You've built your team and competed in matches. Now it's time to push the limits.
This phase teaches you the skills that separate a working agent from a winning one — prompt engineering, memory, tool use, observability, and multi-agent coordination. These aren't just football tricks. They're the same building blocks behind every production agentic system, from customer service bots to autonomous ops teams. The football pitch just makes them visible and fast to iterate on.
Each section teaches a concept, shows you why it matters, walks through a concrete scenario, and gives you an exercise to apply it your way. You don't need to do them all. Pick the one that solves your biggest problem, or work through them in order if you've got the time.
[The Iteration Mindset](#the-iteration-mindset)
Every section in this phase follows the same loop — the same loop used by production AI teams everywhere:

```
Observe → Hypothesize → Change → Deploy → Measure → Repeat
```

The teams that win aren't the ones that follow instructions. They're the ones that understand why things work and can adapt on the fly. The football pitch is your sandbox. The skills are real. Let's go. ⚽
