# Improve Your Agents with AgentCore Features

Right now, your agents are goldfish. Every tick is a fresh start. They don't remember what happened 10 seconds ago, they can't do math, and when something goes wrong you're left guessing why. That's fine for a kickabout in the park. Not fine for a tournament.
Amazon Bedrock AgentCore has building blocks that turn your goldfish into something closer to actual footballers. Each one gives your agents a new superpower:
| Feature | The Superpower | On the Pitch |
| 🧠 Memory | Your agents remember what happened earlier in the match | "Their striker always cuts left — I'll shade that side this time" |
| 🔧 Gateway | Your agents call tactical tools mid-game via MCP | "Pass success to Player 3 is 82%, to Player 4 is 31% — easy choice" |
| 🔍 Observability | You see exactly what your agents are thinking, every tick | "Ah, that's why my defender sprinted forward — the prompt said 'push up when winning'" |

You don't need to do all three. Pick the one that solves your biggest problem, or go through them in order if you've got the time.
