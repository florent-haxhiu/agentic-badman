# AWS Agentic Football Cup

You're about to build a team of AI agents, deploy them to the cloud, and watch them play live football matches — against three AWS-built AI squads — Bedrock Ballers FC (balanced), Total Attack United (extremely aggressive), and Fort Knox Athletic (extremely defensive) — or head-to-head against other teams in the room.

This isn't a typical workshop. There are no pre-baked demos to click through. You'll write real agent code, deploy it to Amazon Bedrock AgentCore, and step into the Player Portal to see your agents compete on a live 3D pitch. Every decision your agents make — pass, shoot, press, defend — comes from the logic you give them.

No AI experience required. No deep coding background needed. AWS Solutions Architects and certified partners will be on hand throughout to help you at every step. Whether you've built agents before or this is your first time, you'll walk out of here having deployed a working multi-agent system and competed with it live.

The workshop is built around three phases:

1. Write your agent code and deploy it to Amazon Bedrock AgentCore with the help of Kiro, an agentic IDE that accelerates every part of the workflow.
2. Set up the Player Portal, register your agents, benchmark against AI opponents, and compete in tournament matches.
3. Learn advanced techniques to iterate fast, improve your agents' decision-making, and dominate the leaderboard.

And when the final whistle blows, you'll take the same skills, tools, and patterns back to your organization, because everything you learn here maps directly to production agentic AI use cases across industries.

Along the way, you'll pick up real skills that go well beyond football:

- How to design agents that reason, coordinate, and adapt in real time
- How to deploy and scale agents using production-grade AWS infrastructure
- How multi-agent systems work — the same patterns behind customer service bots, supply chain automation, and more
- How agentic coding tools like Kiro accelerate every part of the development cycle

## Your Toolkit

You'll work hands-on with a focused set of AWS services and open-source SDKs — the same tools teams use to build production agentic systems today:

- Foundation models that power your agents' reasoning
- A managed runtime to deploy, scale, and monitor your agents
- An open-source framework for building agents with minimal boilerplate
- An agentic IDE that understands your project and helps you build, debug, and iterate on agents through natural conversation

> 💡 Why these tools? They reflect real-world production choices made by AWS customers. By the end of this workshop, you won't just have a football team. You'll have hands-on experience building agentic solutions you can apply to any domain.
